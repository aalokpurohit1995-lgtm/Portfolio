01 // ORIGIN

Curious about people

I’m Aalok Purohit, a Lead UX designer driven by human-centered innovation, behavioral science and strategic problem-solving. Raised in Anand, Gujarat and Rajasthani by origin, I’ve always been drawn to how people think, decide and interact with technology.

02 // TINKERER

Circuits, toys and a million cars

As a kid I modified circuits, took toys apart and sketched cars — I’ve probably drawn over a million by now. That curiosity grew into a career designing intuitive, scalable, high-impact digital experiences.

03 // PRACTICE

Enterprise UX, AI and transformation

Over 8 years I’ve specialised in enterprise UX, AI-driven product design and digital transformation. Today I lead UX at Rolls-Royce; before that I led UX initiatives at WNS.

The work is rooted in data-driven decisions, persuasive design and usability heuristics — making technology functional, engaging and efficient enough that people adopt it and stay.

04 // APPROACH

Clarity, simplicity, impact

I work with persuasion, emotion and trust, alongside cognitive-load reduction and behavioral psychology. Whether it’s a low-friction workflow, sharper user segmentation or better decision-making, the goal stays the same.

  • Behavioral psychology
  • Cognitive load
  • Persuasive design
  • Usability heuristics
  • Trust & emotion
  • Data-driven decisions
05 // BEYOND

Form, function, story

Painting, sketching and model-making keep my eye sharp for form, function and visual storytelling. That cross-disciplinary habit is why the digital work ends up both refined and usable. See some of it below.

Great design balances usability, psychology and business strategy. The goal isn’t usable — it’s indispensable.
